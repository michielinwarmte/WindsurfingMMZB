# Competitive windsurf racers 2006 to 2026: the reference list for the AI characters

Web research by an agent on 6 November 2026 for the 1.0 backlog (epic E10). **This list is the source material only.** The game's characters get altered names (the rule is Q28 in `Documentation/backlog/QUESTIONS.md`); no real sailor's name goes into `Game/`. Spellings are as on the official pages; nationality codes follow sail numbers (ARU Aruba, BON Bonaire, SXM Sint Maarten, BVI British Virgin Islands, ISV US Virgin Islands, GPE Guadeloupe, NC New Caledonia, CPV Cape Verde). "n/f" = not found; gear is given only where a ranking page or report showed it (year in brackets).

## 9. Data caveats

- PWA 2006-2008 women: the official pages are empty; champions and podiums come from contemporary event reports and a forum thread (2007); treat the 2nd and 3rd places as unverified.
- PWA 2020: no rankings exist; the 2026 figures are standings as displayed on 6 November 2026.
- One secondary source names Delphine Cousin as the 2022 PWA slalom champion; the PWA ranking page shows Marion Mortefon, which is used here.
- IFCA: the 2017 and 2019 women's titles, the 2020 and 2023 titles and the 2024 women's fin podium could not be confirmed.
- Défi Wind 2025 winners not found; the 2026 attribution follows the final-day reports.
- Finian Maynard's sail code KV-11 is BVI; the IFCA PDF lists him as USA in 2006.
