# Dossier: the twelve spots as candidate maps, against the Silvaplana baseline

Web research by an agent on 6 November 2026 for the 1.0 backlog (epic E04). Coordinates are launch points from OpenStreetMap/Nominatim unless marked "approx" (map-read, ±0.5 km). Difficulty is 1 to 5 relative to Silvaplana (1 = the same recipe: thermal lake wind, chop only, no tide, open lidar, orthophoto and bathymetry; 5 = tide + swell + shore break or current, and/or no open terrain or bathymetry data). "Not found" means the search did not turn it up, not that it does not exist. Nothing here was verified on the water: it is the starting point for each spot story's task 1 (the dossier) and task 2 (the data).

---

## 1. Lake Garda, Malcesine (Italy)
- **Location:** Launches at Campagnola (45.785 N, 10.821 E), Lido Paina just south of the castle (approx 45.757 N, 10.812 E), Navene/Fraglia Vela water centre 4 km north. Lake, fresh water; the northern basin is ~3.5–4.5 km wide here; a useful map is the Malcesine–Limone–Torbole stretch (~12 × 4 km).
- **Wind:** Two thermal winds. Pelèr from the N: from before dawn to ~11:00, ~20 kn at Malcesine, often 25–30, stronger on the west (Brescia) shore. Ora from the S: from ~12:00–13:00 to sunset, ~15 kn at Malcesine, stronger further north (Torbole 20+). Balin = sudden, unexpected gusts to ~40 kn (hazard). Season April–October. Lee: eastern shore under Monte Baldo (2,218 m) for westerly components; the Ora is weaker at Malcesine than at Torbole. Stations: no live Windfinder station at Malcesine; nearest live station Brenzone (7 km), Riva del Garda (16 km); public networks ARPAV (Veneto) and Meteotrentino (Riva/Torbole). Windguru station IDs: not found.
- **Water:** Fresh, deep (lake max 346 m, steep drop-off), short steep chop in Ora, bigger chop in Pelèr; no tide; summer surface 20–25 °C; pebble beaches, stone quays, small harbour.
- **Beach/launch:** Rig in car parks/beach at Campagnola and Paina; Fraglia Vela Malcesine clubhouse/water centre at Navene; landmarks: Malcesine castle (Scaliger), cable-car line up Monte Baldo, Limone and Campione opposite, Torbole/Riva at the lake head.
- **Events:** No PWA/IFCA slalom at Malcesine found. Fraglia Vela Malcesine runs ~20 regattas/yr (Moth and Contender Worlds 2025, Foiling Week), has iQFOiL/Techno 293 squads. The windsurf racing hub of Garda is Torbole (Techno 293 Worlds 2016, RS:X Youth Worlds 2017, iQFOiL and IFCA fin & foil slalom events), 12 km north; also Campione. Course types: slalom, foil slalom, course racing.
- **Open geodata:** Tinitaly DEM 10 m national (INGV, CC BY 4.0). Three regions meet here: Veneto IDT (DTM 5 m, partial 1 m LiDAR, orthophoto; IODL 2.0 licence), Trentino open data (1 m LiDAR DTM, orthophoto), Lombardy (DTM 5 m, orthophoto); AGEA national orthophoto ~20 cm via regions. Buildings: regional DBT/CTR + OSM (good). Bathymetry: only the IIM 1967 1:50,000 chart is historic; the 2018+ Navy (IIM)/Università Cattolica survey and the CNR-ISMAR GA2017 campaign exist but no open grid was found — expect to request it.
- **Difficulty: 2.** Same physics as Silvaplana (thermal lake, chop, no tide, WindNinja-friendly steep terrain) but two opposing wind systems, three data regions to stitch, bathymetry by request.

## 2. Sotavento, Fuerteventura (Spain)
- **Location:** Lagoon and beach approx 28.06–28.09 N, 14.27–14.30 W (map-read; Nominatim returned nothing for the beach). Atlantic; a 4–5 km beach, lagoon up to 4 km long and up to ~200 m wide at high tide; open sea outside the sandbank.
- **Wind:** NE trade wind (alisio), funnelled between the Jandía mountains (Pico de la Zarza 807 m) and the land behind the beach ("wind funnel"/venturi), plus summer thermal boost: cross-offshore from the left, 15–30 kn on normal days, 35–40+ kn on PWA race days in July/August; gusty near the shore. Best June–September, season April–October. Stations: Windfinder has no live station at Sotavento; AEMET Fuerteventura Airport ~75 km north (station IDs not verified); Windguru ID not found.
- **Water:** Tidal lagoon forms around high tide (35–85 cm deep, a couple of hours either side of HW, biggest around new/full moon); at low tide it empties and you walk ~300 m to the sea. Outside the sandbank: flat to wind chop, small swell; shore break slight. Canary tide range roughly 1–2.7 m (estimate). Water 19–23 °C, turquoise over sand; shore sand.
- **Beach/launch:** Huge sand beach; windsurf centre ~10 min walk north of the Meliá Fuerteventura hotel (kite centre at the hotel); PWA event village/tower on the beach; landmarks: the hotel, Risco del Paso headland (S), Costa Calma (N), the Jandía ridge (W).
- **Events:** PWA Fuerteventura Grand Slam since 1986 (slalom, Slalom X, foil, freestyle), kite world tour; historic speed trials. Course: downwind slalom, Slalom X.
- **Open geodata:** IGN PNOA LiDAR (≥1 pt/m², CC BY 4.0), MDT02/MDT05, PNOA orthophoto 25 cm (CC BY 4.0, "PNOA cedido por IGN"); GRAFCAN/IDECanarias orthophotos ~20 cm and 2 m DTM (licence not confirmed, some products via a sales/registration channel). Buildings: Catastro (CC BY 4.0) + OSM (good). Bathymetry: PNOA LiDAR flown at low tide gives the intertidal lagoon floor; below LW only EMODnet (~115 m) or MITECO coastal "ecocartografía" (coverage to be checked); Navy charts not open.
- **Difficulty: 4.** Tide-driven lagoon that appears and vanishes, a moving sandbank, funnel gusts, chop/swell outside: new tide and dynamic shoreline systems.

## 3. Japan: Tsukuihama, Yokosuka (the main PWA venue)
- **Why this one:** Tsukuihama Beach has hosted the PWA "Fly! ANA" World Cup every year since 2017 (slalom 2017–2019 in May; foil slalom finals in November 2022–2025; 2026 listed late Oct/early Nov). It was the first PWA event in Japan for ~24 years. Omaezaki appears only as a listed wave event (March 2023) and Miyako did not come up at all; Hayama is a club spot.
- **Location:** 35.198 N, 139.665 E (Keikyū Tsukuihama station; beach ~200 m east). Sea: ~1 km sandy beach facing ESE onto Kaneda Bay at the mouth of Tokyo Bay; Bōsō peninsula ~10 km across the Uraga channel.
- **Wind:** No reliable thermal system; synoptic. Pre-frontal SW 12–25 kn (2022 report: SSW 15–19 kn gusting 30; 2026 forecast SW 12–25 kn), post-frontal N/NE; many light-wind event days (2024, 2025 opened windless). Station: JMA AMeDAS Miura, no. 46211, 35.178 N, 139.63 E, 42 m, ~3.5 km away (wind, temp); Windfinder has no live station at Tsukuihama.
- **Water:** Chop, shore break when typhoon swell arrives (2026); semi-diurnal tide ~1.5–2 m springs (estimate); shipping traffic; water ~17–20 °C in November; sand beach, breakwaters and the Kaneda fishing port.
- **Beach/launch:** Miura Windsurf School / "Shallow Reef" on the beach, event tents on the sand, Route 134 behind, low wooded hills (~100 m) of the Miura peninsula.
- **Events:** PWA slalom (2017–2019), PWA foil slalom finals (2022–2025), Japan Windsurfing Association events. Course: slalom, foil slalom.
- **Open geodata:** GSI DEM 5 m (DEM5A LiDAR-based) and 10 m, 1 m in parts (GSI terms, attribution; bulk redistribution needs approval); GSI seamless orthophoto ~20 cm; PLATEAU Yokosuka 3D city model with LOD2 buildings (Government Standard Terms v2.0 = CC BY 4.0 compatible); OSM good. Bathymetry: JODC J-EGG500 (500 m grid, free but "all rights reserved"), JHA M7000 series is commercial: no open fine bathymetry for Kaneda Bay.
- **Difficulty: 4.** Sea with tide, variable synoptic wind with frequent calms, occasional typhoon swell/shore break, no open bathymetry (land data excellent).

## 4. Alaçatı (Turkey)
- **Location:** Windsurf centres along the north shore of Alaçatı Bay, approx 38.26 N, 26.38 E (map-read; OSM town centroid 38.285 N, 26.375 E). Aegean bay ~1.5 × 2 km opening south; standing-depth zone ~500 × 400 m in front of the centres, then the slalom area and open sea.
- **Wind:** Meltem (Etesian) from N–NNW reinforced by the afternoon thermal: side-shore from the left in summer (right in spring/autumn), 15–25 kn, best 18–26 kn, mid-June to mid-September, peaking afternoons; drops out on some days (PWA lay days). Stations: Windfinder has no live station at Alaçatı; nearest live Çeşme 9.4 km; Turkish Met Service (MGM) Çeşme synoptic station; Windguru ID not found.
- **Water:** Flat, waist-deep upwind, chop downwind; tide negligible (~0.2 m); seaweed carpets/shallow patches; 22–26 °C in summer; sandy launch, small shore break.
- **Beach/launch:** Sand beach in front of the centres (ASPC and others; names not confirmed in the search), car parks and cafés at the clubs; landmarks: the low hills around the bay, Alaçatı town windmills (3 km N), Çeşme peninsula, Chios on the horizon.
- **Events:** PWA slalom World Cup (Pegasus Airlines Alaçatı World Cup, ~2007–2013+), PWA Youth/Junior Slalom World Cup 2025–2026 (Alaçatı Windfest); course: 4-gybe downwind slalom finishing on the beach.
- **Open geodata:** No open national DEM/orthophoto download found: HGM (General Directorate of Mapping) products and the TKGM/HGM portals are services for registered users, and a 2020 geospatial-data law restricts collection. Use Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (30 m, open) or ALOS AW3D30; imagery via commercial/Esri basemaps only; OSM moderate (town good, bay shore fair). Bathymetry: none open; EMODnet DTM ~115 m only.
- **Difficulty: 3.** Physics easy (flat, steady thermal, no tide) but poor terrain/ortho data and no bathymetry: the bay floor would have to be hand-modelled.

## 5. Brouwersdam (Netherlands)
- **Location:** 6.5 km dam between Goeree-Overflakkee and Schouwen-Duiveland. Grevelingen-side launches (Zeil- & Surfcentrum, "the pool") approx 51.76 N, 3.86 E; North Sea beach side approx 51.77 N, 3.85 E (OSM dam coordinates 51.762–51.792 N, 3.854–3.883 E). Grevelingenmeer = tide-free salt lake (max ~48 m in the old channel, half of it shallow); North Sea side = open beach with sandbars.
- **Wind:** No thermal; synoptic SW prevailing, best SW–NW, gusty frontal weather and squalls; sailed April–November. Stations: no live Windfinder station on the dam; nearest Stellendam/Haringvlietsluizen 14 km, Neeltje Jans 16 km, Renesse-West 20 km; Rijkswaterstaat Waterinfo "Brouwershavensche Gat 08" (water level, waves); KNMI Wilhelminadorp/Stavenisse ~20–25 km.
- **Water:** Grevelingen: flat pool, chop outside, no tide, waist-deep near the dam; North Sea: waves 0.5–2 m, beach break, tide range roughly 2.3–2.8 m (estimate), currents at the Brouwerssluis inlet; 6–20 °C; brown-green; sand beach on the sea side, rip-rap/basalt on the dam.
- **Beach/launch:** Grevelingen side: surf centre (since 1993), car parks on the dam; sea side: wide beach, beach pavilions, kite zone signposted; landmarks: the dam road N57, Port Zélande marina, Brouwerssluis, Ouddorp lighthouse, Scharendijke.
- **Events:** DAM-X festival (slalom for all levels, freestyle, wing slalom; ~300 riders; 10th edition 9–11 Oct 2026), PWA DAM7 Freestyle World Cup 2013; a Dutch national slalom championship (NK) at the dam was not found.
- **Open geodata:** Excellent: AHN4/AHN5 0.5 m DTM/DSM (CC0), Luchtfoto 8 cm (CC BY 4.0, PDOK), BAG/BGT/3D BAG buildings, Rijkswaterstaat "vaklodingen" (20 m bathymetry grids, open, North Sea/Voordelta via OpenEarth), RWS Grevelingen model bathymetry (D-Flow FM) exists but a public download was not confirmed; 2005/2011 Grevelingen orthomosaics (CC0). OSM excellent.
- **Difficulty: 3 (lake side) / 4 (sea side).** Lake side is Silvaplana-like minus the thermal (synoptic gusts); sea side adds tide, surf and currents.

## 6. Bonaire, Lac Bay / Sorobon (Caribbean Netherlands)
- **Location:** Sorobon Beach 12.092 N, 68.236 W. Lagoon (Lac) ~7 km², sheltered by a barrier reef at its SE mouth (Lac Cai) and mangroves on the north side.
- **Wind:** Trade wind E–ENE (one guide says NE), 15–25 kn (older average ~24 kn), strongest December–August, lighter September–November; onshore/side-on at Sorobon; gustier and more offshore at Lac Cai. No mountains: steady, little shift. Stations: Holfuy no. 1364 "Dunkerbeck Pro Center" on Sorobon beach (12.092 N, 68.236 W; wind, gust, direction, temp); Meteorological Dept Curaçao/KNMI at Flamingo Airport ~8 km; Windfinder "Bonaire/Lac Bay".
- **Water:** Flat, waist-deep (0.8–1.2 m) over sand and seagrass, deepening abruptly in the channel to the mouth; small wind chop; tide ~0.3 m; 26–29 °C; turquoise; sand beach, mangroves, reef breakers visible at the mouth.
- **Beach/launch:** Sorobon: Jibe City, Bonaire Windsurf Place, Dunkerbeck Pro Center, Sorobon Beach Resort, beach bars; kiting banned (kite spot is Atlantis). Landmarks: the reef line, Lac Cai sandbank, mangroves, flat island (highest point 240 m far NW).
- **Events:** PWA Freestyle World Cup finals 2002 (King of the Caribbean), PWA Bonaire 2011 (and ~2009–2014), FORSA Bonaire PWA World Cup 2019, FPT King & Queen of the Caribbean 2023+. Discipline: freestyle (no slalom tour events found).
- **Open geodata:** Weak but improving: DCBD "Elevation and coastal bathymetric model for Bonaire" (improved ASTER DEM 10 m rescaled to 1 m with coastal depth points) and "coastal bathymetry of Bonaire 0–20 m at 2 × 2 m" (dcbd.nl; check Lac coverage and licence); new airborne LiDAR + multispectral flown early 2024 (first ever; a DTM was released; AHN is to be extended to the BES islands) but no PDOK/Kadaster listing confirmed; no open orthophoto confirmed; OSM fair.
- **Difficulty: 2.** Simplest wind model of all (steady trade wind, no terrain), flat shallow water, no tide; only the data side is weaker than Silvaplana.

## 7. Columbia River Gorge, Hood River (USA)
- **Location:** Hood River Event Site 45.7155 N, 121.5126 W, at the Hood River mouth with the big sandbar ("the Spit") into the Columbia; river ~1.5–2 km wide to Bingen/White Salmon (WA). Fresh-water river, Bonneville pool (dam 35 km downstream), so no tide and steady level.
- **Wind:** Westerly gradient wind funnelled through the Cascades gap by the pressure difference between cool Pacific air and the hot interior: W/WSW, 18–25 mph on most summer days, gusts to 40 mph on "nuclear" days, building through the afternoon, June–September (best July–August); easterlies in winter. Stations: Windfinder/iWindsurf sensors (Hood River sandbar, Idlewilde, West Ridge Drive, Hood River airfield 4S2); nearest ASOS KDLS The Dalles ~30 km E; NWS Portland; DOE WFIP2 research tower; USGS gauge Columbia at The Dalles.
- **Water:** River current flows west against the wind, 1–3 kn typical in summer (estimate; stronger in May–June runoff) which stacks steep "Gorge swell" (biggest at Swell City/The Hatchery 5–8 km downstream; the Event Site is choppier); 15–21 °C in summer (estimate); green; sandy spit, rip-rap, lawn.
- **Beach/launch:** Grass rigging lawn, sandy launch, windsurf-only upwind zone, kites from the sandbar; landmarks: Hood River toll bridge (1.3 km upstream), Wells Island (W), I-84 and railway, Mt Hood (S), Mt Adams (N), White Salmon cliffs.
- **Events:** Gorge Cup series (CGRA, 8 races May–August, slalom/course), Gorge Blowout downwinder (Stevenson/Cascade Locks to Hood River, ~14–20 miles, since the early 1980s), Gorge Games (1990s–2000s), US Windsurfing Nationals (held in the Gorge; years not verified); no PWA in recent decades.
- **Open geodata:** USGS 3DEP 1 m LiDAR and DOGAMI Oregon LiDAR (open), WA DNR LiDAR for the north bank, NAIP orthophoto 60 cm (public domain), Microsoft/OSM buildings (good). Bathymetry OPEN: USACE Portland District hydrographic surveys (Hood River reach, e.g. 8 Aug 2020 XYZ) and NOAA NCEI survey F00586 (Hood River multibeam, Dec 2009, BAG/XYZ).
- **Difficulty: 4.** Wind funnel is WindNinja-friendly and data are excellent, but river current plus wind-against-current swell is a new water-physics system (no tide, though).

## 8. Gruissan (France)
- **Location:** Plage des Chalets 43.103 N, 3.117 E. Mediterranean; the Défi course runs 10 km along the coast from Les Chalets across the Ayrolle sandbank to Port-la-Nouvelle (2 out-and-backs = 40 km).
- **Wind:** Tramontane NW–WNW, offshore/side-offshore at Les Chalets (flat "glassy" water), 25–45 kn, 5–10 kn stronger at Port-la-Nouvelle; Défi races need ≥20 kn average; blows all year, Défi in May; gusty with strong "rafales". Marin (SE) is the onshore alternative with waves. Stations: Windfinder live station "Gruissan" (43.106 N, 3.101 E); Météo-France RADOME Narbonne 14 km and Leucate sémaphore 21 km (used as reference for Gruissan forecasts); Windguru ID not found.
- **Water:** No tide (~0.3 m); flat inshore, chop/short swell offshore in Tramontane, shore break with Marin; sand, gentle slope; 15–18 °C in May, ~24 °C in August; blue-green; salt lagoons (Ayrolle, Grazel) behind the beach.
- **Beach/launch:** Rig on the wide sand in front of the chalets on stilts (Gruissan-Plage), Défi village, restricted car access; landmarks: Tour Barberousse in Gruissan village, Massif de la Clape (214 m) NW, Port-la-Nouvelle harbour and wind turbines (S), Narbonne-Plage cliffs (N).
- **Events:** Défi Wind since 2001 (world's biggest windsurf race, ~1,400 riders, long-distance slalom), Défi Kite, Défi Foil/Wing; 2026 edition 14–17 May.
- **Open geodata:** Excellent: IGN RGE ALTI 1 m and LiDAR HD (Etalab 2.0, open), BD ORTHO 20 cm (open), BD TOPO buildings (open); SHOM/IGN Litto3D Languedoc-Roussillon (2009 topo-bathymetric LiDAR, 2011 and 2014–15 marine updates; Etalab 2.0; "not for navigation") covers the Aude coast. OSM excellent.
- **Difficulty: 3.** No tide, no terrain complexity, superb data; the new bits are an offshore gusty wind field over a 10 km-long map and open-sea chop/shore break on Marin days.

## 9. Lüderitz (Namibia)
- **Location:** Speed canal at the south end of the Second Lagoon, 26.687 S, 15.148 E (Wikipedia 26°41′13″S 15°08′53″E; Windy "Lagoon2" 26.683 S, 15.143 E), a few km south of town. Lagoon 1 × 7 km; the canal is a hand-dug trench ~800 m long (500 m timed), "narrow" (width not given in sources), 30–40 cm deep on the windward side to ~1 m leeward, water pumped in before wind days. The bay (Robert Harbour/Lüderitz Bay) in front of the town is deep, cold and choppy; Agate Beach and Grosse Bucht are swimming beaches; no documented bay windsurfing found.
- **Wind:** S–SSW (reports 200–210°), hits the canal at ~140° to the course; 30–50 kn, "good day" ~40 kn, unusable below 30 kn; strongest August–March, event October–November; cold Benguela air with morning fog. Stations: Windfinder live station Diaz Point (8 km); Namibia Meteorological Service synoptic station Lüderitz/Diaz Point; Lüderitz airport.
- **Water:** Flat in the canal (sandbag walls kill chop, water heels ~20 cm across the trench at 40 kn), salty lagoon; sea 12–16 °C; bay: chop, swell at the entrance; sand/silt lagoon, rocky bay shores.
- **Beach/launch:** Canal bank with sandbag wall, timing cameras, container base; landmarks: Shark Island, Diaz Point padrão, the desert dunes, town on the bay.
- **Events:** Lüderitz Speed Challenge 2007–2025 (500 m WSSRC speed; windsurf outright record 53.27 kn Albeau 2015; kite >57 kn); 2026 cancelled (finances), hoped 2027.
- **Open geodata:** None national: no open DEM, orthophoto or bathymetry found; use Copernicus DEM GLO-30/NASADEM (30 m), GEBCO 15″; SA Navy port charts restricted; OSM moderate (town only).
- **Difficulty: 2 (canal) / 3 (bay).** The canal is a trivial geometry (500 m straight, flat) at extreme wind; nothing to model but gust bands. The surrounding terrain would come from 30 m data plus satellite imagery; bay bathymetry would be guesswork.

## 10. Pozo Izquierdo, Gran Canaria (Spain)
- **Location:** Pozo Izquierdo beach 27.825 N, 15.423 W; sailing area ~1 km of coast in front of the village; Atlantic.
- **Wind:** NNE–NE trade wind, side-onshore from the left, accelerated by the east-coast thermal low and the funnel between the island's mountains and the sea: 25–45 kn June–August ("30+ is normal", locally up to 50 kn), peaking 14:00–18:00, lighter mornings; 15–25 kn the rest of the year; gusty. Stations: Windfinder has no live station at Pozo; AEMET Gran Canaria Airport (Gando) 9 km N; ITC (Instituto Tecnológico de Canarias) met masts at Pozo (not public live); Windguru ID not found.
- **Water:** Wind swell 1–3 m (bigger with winter groundswell), rather flat/choppy without ocean swell, shore break onto a boulder/pebble beach, rocky bottom near shore; tide range ~1–2.7 m (estimate); 19–23 °C; deep blue.
- **Beach/launch:** Small stony beach in front of the Centro Internacional de Windsurfing (Club Pozo), promenade, few parking spaces; landmarks: ITC wind turbines and test site just south, Montaña de Arinaga (N), Pico de las Nieves inland, Bahía de Formas.
- **Events:** PWA Gran Canaria Wind & Waves Festival since 1989 (wave; 35th edition 2023), PWA slalom 2009 and 2023 (Slalom X, fin/foil, ~25–30 kn, 1 m waves), Spanish and youth wave events. Course: wave; slalom X when held.
- **Open geodata:** As Fuerteventura: PNOA LiDAR/MDT02/orthophoto 25 cm (CC BY 4.0), GRAFCAN (licence to confirm), Catastro + OSM buildings (good). Bathymetry: no open high-resolution source found (Navy charts closed, EMODnet ~115 m).
- **Difficulty: 5.** Tide + swell + shore break on boulders + 40 kn gusty trade wind + no open near-shore bathymetry: the full set of things Silvaplana does not have.

## 11. Bol, Brač (Croatia)
- **Location:** Zlatni Rat spit 43.257 N, 16.635 E; windsurf schools launch from the beach between the spit and the harbour (approx 43.259 N, 16.642 E). Adriatic, Hvar Channel ~5 km wide to Hvar.
- **Wind:** Maestral, thermal W–NW, side-shore along the south coast, ~13:00–18:00 (strongest ~16:00), 15–20 kn, up to 27–30 kn, funnelled through the Brač–Hvar channel; April–October, best late May–early June and late July–August; Bura (NE, gusty) and Jugo (SE, waves) outside summer; mornings calm. Stations: DHMZ climatological station Bol (43°15′50″N 16°39′14″E, 41 m; public live wind not confirmed); Windfinder live station "Brač/Bol Airport" (plateau, ~10 km, much higher); personal station IBOL3.
- **Water:** Chop and short waves up to 1.5 m in the channel; tide ~0.3 m; deepens fast off the pebble beach; 22–26 °C; crystal clear; pebbles; the spit tip bends east with the Maestral.
- **Beach/launch:** Pebble beach with pine forest behind, windsurf centres, promenade to Bol harbour; landmarks: Zlatni Rat, Vidova Gora (778 m) directly north, Hvar island and Stari Grad bay opposite, Dominican monastery.
- **Events:** PWA World Cup Bol (2019?–2022; 2020 edition 13–18 July; 2021 first on Zlatni Rat, slalom/foil, ~15 races in 6 days), IFCA Slalom World Championship 2025 (~100 racers), European Junior (Techno/iQFOiL) Championships, Croatian nationals. Course: slalom, foil slalom.
- **Open geodata:** DGU digital orthophoto 2019–2024 (DOF5, ~20–25 cm) as open WMS/WMTS (Croatian open licence; download for registered users); national LiDAR 2021–2023 DMR/DMP (publication status unclear; the DMR source is marked "fee"); Copernicus 30 m fallback; OSM good. Bathymetry: HHI (Croatian Hydrographic Institute) not open; EMODnet ~115 m only.
- **Difficulty: 3.** Thermal channel wind is Silvaplana-like, tide negligible, but open-sea chop/short waves, a 778 m mountain right behind the beach, DEM licence unclear and no bathymetry.

## 12. Lago di Santa Croce (Italy)
- **Location:** Lake centre 46.10 N, 12.33 E; launches at Farra d'Alpago beach by Camping Sarathei (NE shore, approx 46.12 N, 12.355 E), Poiatte (E shore mid-lake, approx 46.10 N, 12.35 E) and the sailing club at Santa Croce (S shore). Alpine reservoir 386 m a.s.l., 7.8 km², 6.8 × 2 km.
- **Wind:** Thermal S wind up the Fadalto/Lapisina valley, blowing S→N over the lake (onshore at Farra, side-shore at Poiatte), 5–10 m/s (10–20 kn), 15–25 kn April–July with 20–25 windy days/month, 15–20 kn August–October; rises ~12:00, dies ~19:00 when the sun drops behind the mountains; sudden gust events reported (kite accident). Stations: Windfinder has no live station at Farra (nearest Belluno 11–14 km); ARPAV regional network (nearest station not identified); Windguru ID not found. Max's own project `santa-croce-wind` scrapes vetercek.com (lake station 114, Lago Morto corridor 104) and seven ARPAV stations, with hourly history since 2010.
- **Water:** Fresh, 12–22 °C, green; chop only; no tide but continuous hydro-power level changes (Enel Piave–Santa Croce system; range not quantified); depth 35–44 m max (sources differ); gravel/grass shores, reeds, campsite beach.
- **Beach/launch:** Campsite beach at Farra (windsurf + kite), Poiatte beach by the Baywatch Bar, club/school at Santa Croce; landmarks: Monte Dolada (1,938 m) N, Cansiglio plateau E, Nevegal W, the A27 motorway viaducts along the west shore, Fadalto pass S.
- **Events:** No national championship or slalom event found in searches (only a rowing regatta); known as a freeride/freestyle/kite lake with club regattas; course type unknown.
- **Open geodata:** Veneto IDT (DTM 5 m, partial 1 m LiDAR, orthophoto; IODL 2.0), Tinitaly 10 m (CC BY 4.0), AGEA ~20 cm orthophoto via Veneto, CTR/OSM buildings (good). Bathymetry: none open found (Enel reservoir; INGV seiche study used a depth model); expect to request or sound it yourself.
- **Difficulty: 2.** The closest cousin of Silvaplana (small alpine lake, afternoon thermal, chop, no tide); extras are the reservoir level swing and missing bathymetry.
