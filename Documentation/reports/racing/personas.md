# personas (F4 of AI_FLEET_PLAN.md): the skill sheet, twelve personas, the Race page, the matrix

Built 21 October 2026 (Session 53, the afternoon) by Claude Fable 5.1 itself, on `feature/p9-racing`, while the laptop trained F5's first stage. The names are the lead's choice (decision 7: "the next development may rename them"); F4's Stop is the team's: name them, or keep these.

## What was built

**1. The skill sheet (`Game/race/persona.gd`, `Persona`, a Resource).** D3's numbers from 0 to 1: start timing, start nerve, tacking, gybing, boat speed, upwind sense, downwind sense, gust appetite, risk, aggression, consistency; a style (the start spot, loop or hold, points high or sails low); the gear (the sail it rigs below and above a wind); a name, a sail colour, a home water and a one-line profile. `to_skipper_config(seed, wind)` maps the sheet onto the scripted skipper's character sheet (`SkipperConfig`) between the rookie's end and the all-rounder's and better: the hands' reaction time and noise from boat speed; the pointing, the shift sensitivity, the tacks allowed and the layline margin from upwind sense, tacking and risk; the mark margin from risk; the run's angle from downwind sense; the favoured-end keenness and the hold speed from start nerve; the timing error drawn from a spread of 8 s (timing 0) down to half a second (1), a risky skipper aiming early; the keep-clear distance from aggression (new on the character sheet: `keep_clear_m`). **Consistency** draws every skill a little differently for each race (seeded by the race: the same seed, the same day), so a persona has good days and bad days. A persona never touches the physics (RD7).

**2. Twelve personas (`Game/race/personas/*.tres`).** Lena (slalom specialist, fearless starts, loses the beat), Tobias (course racer, points high, tacks like a machine), Mara (the all-rounder), Jonas (the rookie), Aiko (starts at the pin, goes left, reads the shifts), Sven (loves a breeze), Ines (careful, never over early), Marco (the gambler), Noor (flies downwind, slow to windward), Pavel (holds at the boat end), Elin (the gust hunter), Kofi (the fastest on a good day, inconsistent). `persona:<name>` names one in any race (`tools/race.sh --skippers=persona:lena,persona:tobias`, the game's `--skippers`, the Race page); a `Skipper` with a persona draws its sheet in `setup()` for the race's seed and wind.

**3. The Race page (`Game/ui/race_page.gd`, `.tscn`), behind the menu's two race buttons.** The water (open water, Lake Silvaplana) and the course (T1 and every iQFOiL template at 50 % on the open water; T2 and the course maker's saved courses on the lake); the fleet (3 to 31 boards and you); who sails (the twelve personas, the four characters, both turn and turn about, or the trained brain with the personas: `s<N>_best.json` of the highest stage in `Game/race/brains`); the countdown (30 s, 60 s, 3 min); the wind shadows and the rules on or off. The personas' cards are listed under the rows. The choices are kept for the session. `--page=race` opens it for a screenshot (`.screenshots/p9/race_page.png`).

**4. The matrix (`tools/persona_matrix.sh`).** The twelve personas race each other headless on a windward-leeward (LG2 at 40 %) and a slalom (PD3 at 60 %) at one wind, and their places are put side by side with a note where a persona is three places or more better on one family: the specialists. `template:<ID>@<percent>` is a course name everywhere now (the race tool, the open-water scene), which the matrix and the brains' evaluation on every template use.

**5. Gear per persona (`Persona.rig()`, `--gear=1`, `RaceSetup.gear`).** A persona rigs its sail's area for the wind (a fuller sail on the same rig; the 3D sail is not redrawn). **Off by default, for Michiel:** measured in 12 kt from rest on a reach, after 10 s the 7.8 makes 5.1 m/s against the default 6.5's 6.8 (and a taller rig, luff and boom scaled with the area, was worse still): in this physics a bigger sail only ever loses, so the sail sizes (the sailor's strength against the sail, the sheeting, the camber of a bigger sail) want tuning before the personas carry them.

## Measured

- **Unit tests** (`test_personas.gd`, 5): the twelve load with names, colours and profiles; the sheet maps within the character sheet's ranges, the better skill the better number; the same seed gives the same day and another seed another, and Kofi's reaction time wanders over 40 days where Ines's barely moves; `persona:lena` and `persona:tobias` race headless with their sheets drawn (Tobias points higher, Lena is keener on the favoured end); the gear sets the areas and the physics feels the difference. `test_menus.gd` with the Race page instanced: 5 of 5. 533 of 533 unit tests on the final state of the afternoon
- **A race with four personas in the game** (`--race=t1_loop --skippers=persona:lena,persona:tobias,persona:mara,persona:jonas`): the countdown with Mara, Lena, Tobias, Jonas and you on the HUD, each under its own sail colour (`.screenshots/p9/race_personas.png`).
- **The matrix** (`tools/persona_matrix.sh`, 15 kt, seed 1, run while the laptop trained): the windward-leeward (LG2 at 40 %) went Aiko, Tobias, Marco, Lena, Elin, Noor, Kofi, Jonas, Pavel, Ines, Mara, Sven (12:02 to 14:26); the slalom (PD3 at 60 %) Kofi, Pavel, Sven, Ines, Lena, Jonas, Elin, Marco, Aiko, Noor, Tobias, Mara (3:34 to 5:18). The specialists show: the course's first three are 9th, 11th and 8th on the slalom; the slalom's first three are 7th, 9th and 12th on the course (the tool marks Aiko, Tobias, Marco and Noor as course specialists, Kofi, Pavel, Sven and Ines as slalom specialists, three places or more apart). Two things learnt: Lena, written as the slalom specialist, is 5th and 4th, because the sheet's gybing and tacking skills only set the manoeuvres a skipper allows itself (the gybe itself is the controller's: the same for everyone), so the slalom order comes from boat speed, start nerve and risk; and Mara, written as the all-rounder at 0.7, was 11th and 12th: a 0.7 sheet is well below the all-rounder character (reaction 0.44 s against 0.25, noise 2.0 against 0.5 degrees), so her sheet was raised to 0.85 after the race (and she won the next six-board slalom). Both races ran with the first judge (27 penalties each, every board turning one to four circles), which shaped the order as much as the sheets; the table is to be run again with the tuned judge on a quiet laptop (tools/persona_matrix.sh).
