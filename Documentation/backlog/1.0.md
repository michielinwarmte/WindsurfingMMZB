# What version 1.0 is

Written 6 November 2026 from Max's and Michiel's list for the session of that evening, the audit of the whole project (BOARD.md), the lead's reading of the gaps, and the team's answers of the same evening (QUESTIONS.md). **Everything marked *proposed* waits on an answer in [QUESTIONS.md](QUESTIONS.md).** The detail of each promise is in [epics/](epics/).

**In one paragraph.** A windsurfing racing simulation whose physics you can feel: thirteen real spots (Silvaplana and the twelve of the list) with their own wind and water, the current slalom and free race ranges of the brands under the game's own names with designs close to the real ones, a sailor you learn to control step by step and who is fun on a long straight, fifty AI sailors driven by a trained neural network, with names you remember, who can race any course anywhere by the rules, a walkable beach on every spot that is also the menu, every race format the real tour sails, a season of events like a real tour, and a career from one rig to the top, all racing with friends on a LAN. Fin racing only: foils may come later as a DLC.

## The milestones (no order; the tracks run in parallel)

A milestone is reached when its test passes, whatever else is still open. A release is made from `main` whenever the team wants one; the version number only counts releases (0.2 is the next). Each story's `Milestone:` field names one of these.

| Milestone | Track | The test that says it is reached |
|---|---|---|
| **M1 The sailor** | the 0.2 release | The 6 November work OK'd by Michiel, checked by Max, merged into main and released as 0.2.0 |
| **M2 The race** | racing, controls, the AI's starts | A real spin-out; the wind shadow simulated; contact between boards; the class's rules with tests; an elimination ladder and a course series played; the AI clean on every lake course at every hour with every start within 10 s; the control ladder and the gamepad; a LAN evening on real PCs; the feel checklist filled |
| **M3 The sea** | waves, water, physics | The wave system with the bottom (SWAN), swell with groups, tides and current in the simulation and in the look; the legs that give and the slam fixed; the hull over the hump; the first sea spot sails like the real one by Max's check |
| **M4 The beach** | the world on foot | A first-time player starts on a beach and finds the water within a minute without a menu; the hut, the tower, the coach and five lessons, the beach start, the travel map; the rigged sailor with the manoeuvres' animations; the profile and the save |
| **M5 The gear** | the brands | Every model of the agreed brands traced and numbered under the game's names; the polars per gear and weight against GPS data; the AI picks its gear; every item looked at in the game |
| **M6 The characters** | the roster | The fifty with their names, levels, looks and gear race, and Max remembers the names; the name on the sail, in the standings, on the beach; the fleet heard |
| **M7 The tour** | the season | A season played from the first beach to the final ranking and saved; the day draw and the committee; the long-distance race and the speed run; the legal review done |
| **M8 The career** | the career | A career played through a season by a tester: money, sponsors, gear, the story, progression; the interviews in their agreed form |
| **M9 The brain** | the neural AI | A brain, shaped by its persona, beats the scripted skipper on the yardstick on every spot and format and is the game's default racer, retrained by rule after every physics change |
| **M10 The spots** | the map track | Thirteen spots pass the ten checks (counted n of 13 on the board); the spot abstraction and the generic pipeline exist; Silvaplana completed with its details |
| **M11 Performance and release** | tech and project | Fifty boards at 60 fps with the full physics (or the measured maximum written here); CI; the release pipeline; the download within budget; crash reports; the store page and trailer if distributed |

The number of releases between 0.2 and 1.0 is open: a release goes out whenever a milestone or a good batch is ready (the team's call, no dates).

## Out of scope for 1.0 (proposed, Q4)

Foiling (decided: a DLC later at most), wave sailing and jumping, freestyle, wing and kite, VR, consoles, Mac, a web build, mod support, online leaderboards, live weather from the internet. Internet multiplayer and Linux: decided in Q4.

## How this file changes

An answer in QUESTIONS.md changes a line here and the epics it touches; the session that moves it writes the date and the answer in the epic's Notes and strikes the question through. A new promise becomes a new epic (E19 next) and a row here; a new milestone gets the next number (M12).
