# Silvaplana: spot notes

Local knowledge from Max, for the map track ([MAP_SILVAPLANA_PLAN.md](MAP_SILVAPLANA_PLAN.md)). AI sessions: read this before M2, M3 and M4, and treat it as the reference for how the spot should look and feel. Max: answer in your own words, short is fine, and leave out what you don't know. Photos can go in `Art/silvaplana/reference/`.

## The launch
- Where exactly do you rig up and launch? (Camping beach? Which part?)
- What is the ground like: grass, gravel, sand? How steep is it into the water?
- How deep is it at the water's edge, and how far out do you wade before you can sail?

## The wind
- Where on the lake is the Maloja strongest, and where is it weakest or gusty?
- Where are the wind holes or lee areas (under which shore, behind what)?
- How do the gusts feel: how often, how much stronger, do they come in patches you can see?
- How much does the direction swing, and how quickly?
- What does a typical session look like: when does it start, peak, and die?

**Max, 15 October 2026** (answering the questions about `Documentation/images/maloja/pattern_game.png` in [MALOJA_WIND_RESEARCH.md](MALOJA_WIND_RESEARCH.md)), in his words, lightly edited:
- Weaker along the Corvatsch (south-east) shore and at the Sils end: "pretty good".
- The wind blows along the lake: "pretty good as well".
- "On the north part, as you've drawn it, there is a little more wind, so that is good."
- In the south, "around the little corner", every so often a gust comes that is as strong as in the strongest parts.
- "The gusts are very noticeable on light days, and a little bit less on very windy days." On the whole lake, but more gusts at the starting (Sils) end; further downwind it gets a little more stable.
- "The little corner" is the bend of the south-east (Corvatsch, forest) shore, around the middle of the lake on the Surlej side.
- A gust patch is about 100 to 200 m across.
- How often a gust comes and how long it lasts: all of it, randomly: sometimes every 10 to 30 seconds for a few seconds, sometimes every minute or so for 5 to 15 seconds, sometimes every few minutes for half a minute or more.
- Does the wind turn in a gust? "Depends on where you are", the common lake-sailing knowledge (which side of the gust you are on, and how close to a shore).
- The strength on the water, when the station at Segl-Maria reads its usual 12 to 13 kt: "depends on the day"; a typical competition day can be 12 to 14 kt, 15 to 18 kt or 18 to 22 kt.
- What wins upwind in a course race: pressure, shifts and shore effects, all of them ("hard question").
- The better side upwind: usually the north-west side (the Julier road / village side).
- Whether it turns left in the evening as it dies: doesn't know.

- How cold is the water in summer, roughly?

## Gear
- Which sail and board do you use there in a typical Maloja, and how much bigger is that than what you would use in the same wind at sea level? (The air at 1791 m is about 20 % thinner, so a sail pulls less; see PHYSICS_REQUESTS_FROM_MAP.md, R2.)

- What do you see from the launch beach: buildings, the camping, the road, the trees?
- Anything typical about the light or the sky in the afternoon?
