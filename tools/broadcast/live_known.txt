KNOWN (the night of 27 to 28 November 2026)
-------------------------------------------
What works
- The week runs by itself, heat after heat, with the ladder, the points (a win 0.7, the dropped
  results after 4 and 7 eliminations) and the standings, and is saved after every heat.
- The TV director films it with the stream's measured numbers; the commentator has about 740 of
  the real commentator's lines (from the 27 h of streams and the eleven heat replays, judged by
  hand), and for the event's own moments 39 of his sentences and 47 lines written for the game
  (the heat called, who goes through, who is out, the elimination won, the standings).
- Heats at 30 knots take the winner 193 to 207 s (the real leader: about 187 s). With the 60 s
  countdown the eight cross the line 0 to 18 s after the gun.

What is not right yet
- The AI's gybes: a board loses most of its speed at every mark (about 5 s a mark against the
  real sailors), and at mark 1, where the whole fleet arrives together, one to three of the eight
  are pushed inside the mark and cannot get back. This is AI training for Sotavento (Max: "yes but
  later"), not built this night. What the lead did set: every event sailor passes a mark at least
  14 m out (measured: no more missed marks further round the course; --event_mark_margin=0 in a
  launcher gives each sailor his own margin again).
- Because of that, a rule of the lead's (not the tour's): a sailor gives up his heat ("retired")
  when the heat's fourth board rounded the mark he is still sailing for more than 45 s ago.
  Before, such a board circled its mark for minutes. Off with --event_give_up=0 in a launcher.
- Rarely (5 times in about 90 test heats) a board's physics breaks: its turning speed goes to
  infinity in one step. Twice that hung a heat. The race now puts such a board back at its start
  spot and retires it (one warning in the log), so the broadcast goes on. Why the physics does
  it is not known: a question for Michiel.
- The ranking decides little yet: the provisional sailors keep their characters' daring, and a
  daring tail-ender often wins his heat. The roster is Max's to make.
- The wind is 30 knots and steady in every heat. The real week had 30, 30, 35, 40 and 28 knots
  (the commentator's words, in the schedule file); the AI has never been tried above 30. In 25
  knots the heats were worse than in 30 (two test heats), so less wind is no way round the marks.
- Every heat is the same eight boards with other sailors: the next heat does not wait on the water
  yet (the plan's 16 boards), and you cannot sail your own heats yet.
- The 64 names are provisional (made up); the real names are Max's to make.
- The start boat is on the upwind end of the line and the wind axis of the course is 025: the
  lead's reading of the stream, for Max to confirm.
- A sailor over the line early goes back and starts again; in the real event he is out of the heat.
- The voice's licence allows play tests, not a public release (its base voice is research-only).
- Not checked by a person: how the voice sounds, how loud it is against the wind and the water, and
  how the names are said.
