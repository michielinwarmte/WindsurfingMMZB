# Changelog

What each version of the Windsurfing Simulator contains, newest first. The version number lives in `Game/project.godot` (`config/version`) and the start menu shows it. "Session n" refers to the entries in [Documentation/PROGRESS_LOG.md](Documentation/PROGRESS_LOG.md), where every session is written up in detail.

Version numbers: `0.x` while the team still changes the physics freely. A patch (`0.1.1`) is fixes only; a minor (`0.2.0`) adds features. How a release is made is in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

## Next version (0.2, not released yet)

- **GP0, the push and the body measured before they are built** (Session 56, 26 October 2026; [the report](Documentation/reports/gameplay/gp0_measure.md); on the branch `gp0/measure`, not merged into `main` yet): `tools/push_check.sh` sails the player's beginner controller headless with the autopilot's margin or the sailor's technique targets set per case, on the game's open water with waves and gusts. About 600 cases. It found the dial's three settings, that easing for the gusts beats both the safe and the reckless sailor at 24 kt, and the mouse's ranges; the fin does not warn before a catapult, and the round-up comes only in the last half second. From Max's answers and his play it found six things for the physics: the open sea's gusts jump every period (PH24), the sailor reacts instantly (PH25), light wind is too weak (PH26), the leeward rail costs speed when planing (PH27), the rigid sail cannot be sheeted in because it has no twist (PH28), and the rider's body breaks beyond its numbers (PH29). `tools/play_push.sh` is a playtest of the pushed sailor in 24 kt with gusts: the F1 panel shows the feet's overload and the fin live and freezes them from before each fall. Nothing in `Game/sim/` changed.
- **Gusts you can see coming (PH7, GR2)** (Session 63, 31 October 2026; on the branch `ph7/gust-patches`, from `gp1/push-slider`): the open water's gusts are patches of stronger and lighter air, fronts across the wind and lanes along it, rolling downwind with the air, the lake's measured pattern with the settings' gust strength as the spread; they are drawn on the water around the board, a gust darker and matt, a lull glassier, so you can ease before a gust reaches the sail (PHYSICS_SPEC.md 8.3b). The older sine gusts stay for the tests and the measurements before this date.
- **The board at the limit, heard and seen, the sailor's view and the close camera (SO1, GR1, UI1, GR4)** (Session 63; on the branch `gpa/feedback`): the fin hums at speed, louder as its lift nears the stall and stuttering over the top, and the rig creaks as the sailor hikes out to the limit or is overloaded toward the sail; the spray grows with the fin's load and the heel, and the board and rig chatter near the fin's limit; the strip shows the push and the forecast wind only (the setting "Sailor's view"); the follow camera sits low behind the board looking at the water ahead (the setting "Close camera").
- **GP11 measured** (Session 63; [the report](Documentation/reports/gameplay/gp11_broad_reach.md)): from rest the board planes on courses up to 120° at 18 kt and sits on the hull's hump deeper, whatever the sailor's technique: the physics' planing onset (PH16) seen from the broad reach, not the controller's.
- **A time trial (GP4, GD5)** (Session 63; on the branch `gp4/time-trial`): the Race page's fleet "You alone: a time trial" races you alone against your own best on that course and wind, kept in your user folder; the splits show the gap to your best run and the finish says whether you beat it.
- **The gear: the game's own brands, their boards, sails and fins, and a Gear page (GE1 to GE4)** (Session 64, 1 November 2026; on the branch `gear/lead`, from `gp4/time-trial`): twelve made-up brands after the sport's makers with their logos; 22 boards, 36 sails and 5 fins shaped and coloured after the makers' 2025/2026 slalom and free race gear, every one with the real sizes' physics numbers; a Gear button on the main menu with Board, Sail and Fin tabs and a turning preview; the choice is kept and sailed on both waters and in races. The default board and sail keep their numbers and wear their brand's livery. The sail sizes measured again: with each sail's own numbers the 8.0 is faster than the 6.5 in 12 kt and the 6.5 faster from 18 kt (PH14).
- **The AI fleet's training on the whole range of conditions, with its own push (RA5, RA12, RA13)** (Session 64; on the branch `race/train-all`): every stage draws the settings' winds, seas (to a 0.6 m crest), gust patches and shifts; the brain sets the push slider itself and sees the sea and the gusts; the player and the fleet sail in the same gusts (one wind clock per race); a trainer bug fixed (a worker sailed one course in one wind for a whole stage). The nights ran on Michiel's PC (31 October to 1 November 2026): the six stages' brains are in `Game/race/brains/` (`all_s<n>_best.json`), but they are not race-ready yet, they change tack every 10 to 15 s ([nights_review.md](Documentation/reports/fleet_gear/nights_review.md)); the Race page's brain option keeps `s1_best`.
- **The AI sailors faster around T1 and T2, twenty characters from rookie to pro, the start, and the trainer's tack discipline (RA6, RA11, RA14, RA15, RA16, RA18, RA20, GE5)** (Session 66, 1 to 2 November 2026; on `gear/lead`; [the plan](Documentation/reports/fleet_speed/PLAN.md)): the scripted skipper beats and runs at the angles measured for its own board in each wind (a polar per wind, `Game/config/skipper_polar.tres`), gets back on the plane after a gybe in light wind, no longer circles a mark it passed on the wrong side, and eases down for a finish line its track would miss; the four characters finish every race with no falls and the sum of their times is 4.6 % lower (T1 at 12 kt from 17:02 to 13:52 for the all-rounder). The learned skipper waits 20 s between manoeuvres, trains on T1 and T2 (the stages C1 and C2) against the all-rounder's own numbers, and the brains are named by their run. Twenty personas from rookie to pro (eight new: Hana, Bruno, Liv, Yusuf, Greta, Tomas, Ravi, Pia), each with a push level, a light-wind or strong-wind skill and its own brand's board, sail and fin from the catalogue, sized for the wind (the Race page's "Their own gear"); a character pushes only off the wind; a board heading the wrong way round a mark goes back down the leg first. The AI boards start within seconds of the gun in every wind (the start plan at the polar's angle; in 12 kt on T1 they were a minute late), the race's time limit fits the course and the wind, and a penalty turn eases down before it turns.
- **The waves and the wind, the menu, the push in a race** (Session 67, 2 November 2026; on the branch `gp/sea-follows-wind`; [the report](Documentation/reports/waves/waves_direction.md)): a new "Wind from" or wave setting in the pause menu rebuilds the sea under the board right away, so the waves line up with the wind you sail in (before, they kept the old direction until R; in a race the new sea still waits for R). The chop can come from a compass bearing of its own, like the swell ("Chop comes from", "Chop from" on the Waves tab), so a lake's sea keeps running along the lake while the wind veers. "Wave spread" sets how far the waves fan out around their direction; its default is narrow (the chop within ±14° of downwind), where the old sea sent them up to 55° off and looked as if the waves ignored the wind. The settings menu no longer sets the graphics again at every slider step (it stuttered). The race block shows the push slider ("push 75 %") and names Up/Down.
- **The waves follow the wind, a race block and a push gauge** (Session 68, 3 November 2026; on `gp/sea-follows-wind`): the water's small wind ripples lie across the wind whatever its direction (they kept the East-West axes, so in a North or South wind the waves you saw ran 90° off it; [the report](Documentation/reports/waves/waves_direction.md), section 7). The race's top-left block is a card in the menus' look with the place and the clock large and the fleet as a table (the leaders and the boards around you in a big fleet); the push slider is a gauge at the bottom, coloured from cruising to a crash likely, in free sailing and in races. A maximized window stays maximized when a setting changes. A race of personas no longer loads the whole gear catalogue some thirty times as it starts (the graphics card ran out of memory). **The AI fleet starts again in the exported game:** it pointed into the wind and never started on T1 and T2, because the export dropped the numbers its skippers plan with (the polar); the game now ships its resource files as text.
- **0.2.0-beta.1 (2 November 2026):** a beta build of everything above up to Session 67, from the branch `gp/sea-follows-wind`, zipped as `WindsurfingSimulator-0.2.0-beta.1-windows.zip` for the team's play-tests. Not a release on `main`. **On hold** (2 November, evening): in Max's play-test the waves still run 90° off the wind (ROADMAP GR8); the GitHub pre-release is prepared and not published.
- **0.2.0-beta.2 (3 November 2026):** a test build of everything above up to Session 68, from the branch `gp/sea-follows-wind`, in `.release/WindsurfingSimulator-0.2.0-beta.2/` and zipped as `WindsurfingSimulator-0.2.0-beta.2-windows.zip` for Max's play-test (the waves in a North wind, the race card and the push gauge, the maximized window, the AI start, which beta.1 also had broken). Not a release on `main`; the release build ran a race of seven personas and the player at 221 fps on Max's laptop.

## 0.1.0 — 21 October 2026

The first release: the state of the game after Session 53, merged into `main` from `feature/p9-racing` (which carried U1, U2, Phase 9 and the AI fleet's F1 to F5). The first version that can be downloaded and played without Godot.

### The game, rebuilt in Godot (Phases 0 to 6; Sessions 28 to 38, 28 September to 16 October 2026)

- **The physics specification** written from the Unity version's code and documents, every model with its formula, inputs, units, coefficients and source, and the legacy contradictions resolved (Session 29; [Documentation/PHYSICS_SPEC.md](Documentation/PHYSICS_SPEC.md)).
- **The core simulation**, headless and tested: a 6-degree-of-freedom rigid body with fixed substeps, the force models one at a time with tests (buoyancy, hull drag and planing lift, fin, sail and apparent wind, rake steering, the sailor's weight), `WindsurferSim.step()` and a `Telemetry` snapshot (Session 30).
- **The playable prototype**: the input map, four cameras (follow, orbit, top-down, free), the telemetry HUD, a sky and water (Session 30). Then, from the first play-tests: falls instead of capsizes, downwind trim, the helm (rake, rig tilt, rail), beginner assists, a hull that carves when banked, tacks and gybes on Space and on the keys, trim by feel, getting out of irons, sheeting to the pull, sailing in 24 kt, and the manoeuvre matrix `tools/matrix.sh` (Sessions 31 to 34).
- **The autopilot, the validation suite and the tuning**: `tools/test.sh validation` with its sailing checks, and the polar at 15 kt (Session 35; [Documentation/POLAR_15KT.md](Documentation/POLAR_15KT.md)).
- **Waves and the ocean**: a Gerstner wave field, the ocean shader (vertex displacement, depth colour, Fresnel reflection, sun glint, foam) and a water mesh that follows the camera; the validation on a swell-dominated sea (Session 36). The sailor became a body of their own and the rig is held by the sailor (Session 37).
- **The rig's visuals**: board, sail, boom, mast and fin as Blender models built from the configuration's sizes by `tools/blender/build_rig_models.py`, a sail that bends with the simulation, the sailor figure on a fixed-length skeleton; the start, settings and pause menus, the controls and how-to pages (Session 38).

### Lake Silvaplana, the first real map (the map track; 14 to 16 October 2026)

- **M0 to M3**: the tools and the Swiss open data (the terrain, the lakebed, the 10 cm aerial photos), the terrain and the lakebed in Godot, the look (the photos, the sky, the mountains, see-through water, then realistic lake water), and sailing on the lake: the start off the camping beach, the thin air at 1,791 m, fresh water, the shore stop, the mini-map ([Documentation/MAP_SILVAPLANA_PLAN.md](Documentation/MAP_SILVAPLANA_PLAN.md)).
- **The wind analyser, W0 to W4a and W5**: the research into the Maloja wind ([Documentation/MALOJA_WIND_RESEARCH.md](Documentation/MALOJA_WIND_RESEARCH.md)), the fixed pattern over the terrain (direction from WindNinja, strength from satellite photos), the 3D wind viewer, gusts and lulls measured on the photos, pulses, shifts and the day's cycle, the race course maker with the ten iQFOiL templates, and the map's wind connected to the sailing (`MapWind`, with the time of day on the HUD).
- The physics and the map tracks merged on 16 October 2026.

### Environment, effects and sound (Phases 7 and 8; Session 39, 16 October 2026)

- One water shader for the sea and the lake, the sky and the haze with the Silvaplana light, spray from the rails, wake and foam behind the tail, a splash on landing, the red streamer at the masthead, the orange wind flag on a buoy, the force arrows (F3), gust patches on the lake from the map's wind.
- Every sound made from noise at startup and shaped by the simulation: the wind, the water (lapping, the planing hiss, splashes) and the sail (flapping, the crack when it fills), on three audio buses with sliders on the Audio tab.
- Waves by group (swell and chop), the board's shape, and the first sailing on Silvaplana in the game.

### Play-test upgrades (U1 and U2; Sessions 46 to 49, 19 October 2026)

- **U1, planing on a smaller wet area**: the porpoising fixed with a 1 degree tail vee and the flat tail, the stance and the rig's lean, the gust calibration; the wet length measured and reported in the telemetry ([Documentation/archive/PLAYTEST_UPGRADES.md](Documentation/archive/PLAYTEST_UPGRADES.md), [U1_PROGRESS.md](Documentation/archive/U1_PROGRESS.md)).
- **U2, the fin bends**: the fin as a bending beam with a measured stiffness (the IST Lisbon flex test), the heeled hull's lift fixed (the cause of the long wet part), the sailor's technique, the bending fin drawn, the fin's push as an arrow in the force view; the sailor riding the fin and the hull's planing lift against Savitsky studied and reported ([U2_PROGRESS.md](Documentation/archive/U2_PROGRESS.md)). Max: "feels quite nice now".

### Racing (Phase 9; Sessions 50 to 53, 19 to 21 October 2026)

- **A race you can sail** (9A): `RaceSim` (the countdown, the gun, OCS, the roundings by geometry checked against all ten iQFOiL templates, the clock, the splits, the results), the courses on the water (buoys, the committee boat, the pin, the lines, the mini-map), `CourseFitter` and the course maker's "Fit to water", the T1 training loop and the T2 sprint slalom, the race buttons in the menu, the racing HUD, R to race again ([Documentation/archive/RACING_PLAN.md](Documentation/archive/RACING_PLAN.md), [P9_PROGRESS.md](Documentation/archive/P9_PROGRESS.md)).
- **Scripted skippers** (9B): the `Skipper` (laylines, tacks and gybes, the timed start, keeping clear) with four characters, `tools/race.sh` and `tools/race_matrix.sh`; all four finish both courses at 12, 18 and 24 kt.
- **The learning harness** (9C): `RaceObservation`, `NeuralNet`, `RaceEnv`, `LearnedSkipper`, the evolution trainer `tools/race_evolve.sh` on all cores, and the first night brains.
- **The AI fleet, F1**: the release build (`tools/release.sh`), 3 substeps for every board (Michiel's decision), the sea query at half the cost with identical answers, the physics step without the engine's locks (`no_wait`, bit for bit the same), the race runner's batched worker tasks (`fleet_frame`): 20 AI boards and the player at 60 fps, measured with `tools/fleet_measure.sh`; the thread bench that found the global engine lock.
- **F2, the living skipper**: states driven by the race (free sailing in an area, the pre-start with the line bias and the end choice, loop or hold back, the OCS return, racing, finished), boards spawning anywhere, the countdown gathering the fleet.
- **F3, real life**: wind shadows through one hook in the wind fields (spec 8.11), the racing rules (`RaceRules`: port and starboard, windward and leeward, clear astern, room at the mark) with the penalty turn or 20 s, in the game, the race tool and the trainer.
- **F4, personas**: the skill sheet (`Persona`), twelve personas, `persona:<name>` in any race, the Race page in the menu, `tools/persona_matrix.sh`, `template:<ID>@<percent>` as a course name; the gear per persona built but off (a bigger sail is slower in this physics, for Michiel).
- **F5, the trainer**: `SailingDistance` as the reward, `RaceEnv` v2 with drops and respawns, `RandomCourses`, the curriculum S1 to S6 (`TrainingDrops`), the TCP bridge to Python, PPO with `tools/train.sh` (`pip install torch stable-baselines3`), the brain export with its parity test, the evaluation against the all-rounder, and `tools/gens.sh` to watch a night's generations race. The first PPO brains of stage S1 are in `Game/race/brains/`.

## Before 0.1: the Unity version (December 2025 to September 2026; Sessions 1 to 27)

The game began on 19 December 2025 as a Unity project in C#: the physics simulation, the controller, the UI, Blender models of the board and sail, and in Session 27 ideas for an ocean shader, Gerstner waves, sail cloth, spray and procedural audio that were never compiled or played. It was never released. It is kept, read-only, in [`Legacy/`](Legacy) with its own documentation and progress log; several of its sign-convention tables are wrong, and the Godot version derives its conventions afresh.
